Fresh Food for Everyday Meals

GreenBite is a fresh food restaurant in Chennai built around simple, lighter meals that still feel satisfying. The menu focuses on bowls, salads, smoothies and desserts made for diners who want something wholesome without making the meal complicated.

The GreenBite style is straightforward: fresh ingredients, honest flavors and food that is easy to enjoy whether you are stopping in for a quick lunch, choosing a lighter dinner or planning a relaxed meal with friends.

What You Can Order

Bowls

Choose fresh combinations like the Green Goddess Bowl or Power Protein Bowl, with greens, grains, vegetables and house-style dressing.

Salads

The salad options keep things crisp and direct, from seasonal vegetables and herbs to a Classic Caesar with parmesan and creamy dressing.

Smoothies & Desserts

GreenBite also serves smoothies with fruits and greens, along with cheesecake desserts for visitors who want a sweeter finish.
